Privacy Policy

What the bot accesses, what it keeps, who can see it, and how to contact us about your information.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

1. Who operates this project

Destiny 2 Event Manager is an independent community project operated by EZMehrtenz. For questions, access or correction requests, or requests to delete your information, contact d2embot@outlook.com.

This policy describes the current development version of the bot and its project website. Features described as previews may use sample data rather than live records.

2. Information accessed or stored

  • Discord identity: your user ID and, where recorded, username and display name. Discord avatars may be fetched to render panel headers.
  • Server registration: server ID, registration status, timestamps and the original server nickname used when attempting to restore it on unregistration.
  • Bungie link: the full Bungie name you submit, matching membership identifiers and membership type. The link can be reused across servers.
  • Requests and events: requester and claimant IDs and Bungie names, selected activity and difficulty, experience or requirements, availability, date/time and time zone, additional notes, workflow status, participant records and activity-access warnings. Channel/message IDs and change timestamps support updates and recovery.
  • Server configuration: trusted role mappings, game-role settings, category/channel destinations, published catalogue snapshots, panel IDs and the ID of the administrator who saved settings.
  • Installations and abuse prevention: server ID/name, owner Discord ID, whether the bot is present, join/departure and last-seen timestamps, last interaction time, aggregate interaction and unhandled-error totals, current block status and private block/unblock audit entries with operator ID, reason and timestamp.
  • Runtime information: Discord member IDs and role membership used for role assignment, access checks and counters; activity availability returned by Bungie; temporary interaction sessions; and operational/error logs that can contain user, server, channel or ticket identifiers and technical error details.
  • Support correspondence: information you choose to send by email when requesting help or exercising data rights.

Whole-server member and role data used for counters is kept in runtime memory; we do not maintain a separate database of every Member for this purpose. Player identity and participation records listed above are stored when their corresponding features are used.

The bot does not request your Discord or Bungie password. Current Bungie linking uses a name lookup, not Bungie OAuth. The application does not request Discord Presence or Message Content intents and does not routinely archive ticket-channel conversations. Information entered into bot forms is different from ordinary channel chat and is processed as described above.

3. Why information is used

We use this information to connect the Discord identity you provide with a Bungie profile, remember server registration, manage optional D2 roles and nicknames, apply server permissions, coordinate Sherpa requests and events, show participation and game-role counts, check available activity data, restore interrupted workflows and investigate errors or support requests.

Where applicable data-protection law requires a lawful basis, providing requested functionality and the legitimate interests in operating, securing and troubleshooting this community service are the purposes relevant to these activities. The precise basis can depend on the request and your location; contact us for questions or objections.

Installation metadata and aggregate usage help us operate the service, investigate misuse and enforce server access restrictions. App operators can block a server and make the bot leave. Server tracking does not collect ordinary conversations or maintain a per-member command history.

4. Visibility and service providers

Ticket details and event posts are sent to Discord. Other Members with access to the relevant channels can see the information shown there, including full Bungie names, submitted notes, schedules, organisers, rosters and access warnings. Server administrators control channel permissions; a publicly visible request is not a confidential support conversation.

The bot communicates with Discord for identity, membership, roles, avatars and messages, and with Bungie for player lookup, activity definitions and availability. Player lookup sends the Bungie name you supply to Bungie; activity checks use the linked membership identifiers. Persistent bot records are currently held in a Neon-hosted PostgreSQL database. The operator’s email provider, Microsoft Outlook, processes support correspondence. These providers process information under their own applicable policies and service arrangements.

The operator and people specifically authorised to administer its infrastructure may access stored records to operate the bot and handle requests. An in-server Bot Admin role is not, by itself, permission to access the database or bot credentials. We do not sell user information or use it for targeted advertising. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

5. Retention and unregistration

Registration and the reusable Bungie link persist between bot restarts. Historical requests, event participation and server settings also persist. The current implementation has no automatic expiry or deletion schedule for these database records; a completed or cancelled workflow does not automatically erase its history. Temporary preview/editor sessions expire or are discarded when the bot restarts.

/unregister removes only your registration in the server where you run it. It keeps the shared Bungie account link and does not erase historical requests, event records or participation. Removing D2 likewise does not delete these records.

We review retained data when handling deletion requests and when information is no longer needed for the stated service purposes. Contact us to request removal rather than relying on unregistration as a complete deletion process. Any legally required retention, or limitations involving service-provider logs, backup copies or content held by others, will be explained when relevant to your request.

Installation records and abuse-prevention audit entries currently have no automatic expiry. They may remain after the bot leaves a server or access is blocked, so that restrictions persist and reviews can be handled. Unblocking removes the current restriction but retains its audit history. Requests to delete these records are reviewed separately, including any continuing need to prevent abuse.

6. Access, corrections and deletion

Email d2embot@outlook.com with the subject “Data request — Destiny 2 Event Manager”. Include your Discord user ID, the server concerned if relevant, and whether you want access, correction, deletion or another review. Do not post these requests in a public game-ticket channel.

We may ask you to confirm control of the relevant Discord account before disclosing or changing its information. Do not send passwords, tokens, authentication codes or identity documents unsolicited. Requests are handled manually; there is currently no self-service command that erases all linked data.

Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to object to or restrict processing, obtain portable information, or complain to a relevant data-protection authority. We will respond to applicable requests within the required legal timeframes.

Removing a database record does not automatically delete every existing Discord message, quoted copy or content held by another person. Tell us which bot posts you want reviewed; content managed by Discord or a server administrator may also require their assistance.

7. Website privacy

This website contains static pages. It has no sign-in, tracking scripts, advertising, analytics code, contact forms or cookies added by this project. Its fonts and visual assets are served locally with the site. Clicking an email link opens your email application; it does not submit a form here.

The public site is intended for GitHub Pages hosting. GitHub documents that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed into GitHub. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its handling of that information. External websites and email services have their own privacy practices.

8. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its date when relevant data practices change, including material changes to hosting or retention. Contact d2embot@outlook.com if you need clarification about the current version.
