1. About the service

Destiny 2 Event Manager is a Discord bot for coordinating Destiny 2 community activities. It is in active development. Its current features include game-role selection, Bungie-name registration, server role configuration, Sherpa requests and linked event participation. Some interfaces are explicitly marked as previews and do not create real requests or events. Broader independent LFG workflows are still being developed.

These terms cover the bot and this project website. Discord and Bungie operate their own services under their own terms. This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Bungie or Discord.

2. Your account and conduct

You must meet Discord’s age and eligibility requirements and follow Discord’s terms, Bungie’s applicable rules and the rules of each server you use.

  • Use your own Discord account and provide accurate information. Do not impersonate another player or link someone else’s Bungie identity.
  • Do not use the bot for harassment, spam, abuse, unauthorised access or disruption.
  • Do not submit passwords, authentication codes, payment details or other sensitive information in ticket notes or support messages.

Bungie registration currently looks up the full Bungie name you provide. It does not use Bungie OAuth or prove that you own the matching game account.

3. Roles, registration and visibility

Choosing the D2 game role is separate from linking a Bungie identity. Server owners decide which roles grant Sherpa and Bot Admin capabilities and control channel visibility through Discord permissions. An owner may use an existing role-management bot instead of our role panel.

Registration may change your server nickname to your full Bungie name where Discord permissions allow. Unregistration attempts to restore the recorded original nickname. These changes may fail because of nickname length, role hierarchy or Discord restrictions.

Sherpa request channels are currently designed to be visible to Members. Request details, notes, Bungie names, schedules, access warnings and event participation may be visible to others with channel access. Review the displayed audience before submitting.

4. Requests and events

A request does not guarantee a Sherpa, a place in a fireteam, an activity completion or any in-game reward. Coordinate dates, time zones and requirements with the other participants. You remain responsible for checking your own game access.

Activity and difficulty information comes from Bungie and may be incomplete, outdated or unavailable. A bot warning or availability result is guidance rather than a guarantee of launch eligibility.

Claiming a Sherpa request assigns responsibility; settlement creates its scheduled event after confirmation. Joining, leaving, cancelling and completing an event follow the permissions and controls available in that server.

5. Development and availability

The bot is currently offered without a charge from this project. It may be restarted, interrupted, changed or withdrawn during development. Availability also depends on Discord, Bungie, the database provider and the machine running the bot. We do not promise uninterrupted service or successful delivery of every interaction.

We may restrict misuse and change features or these terms as the project develops. Changes will be posted here with an updated date. These terms do not exclude rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded.

6. Privacy and deletion

The Privacy Policy explains the data the bot accesses, stores and displays. /unregister removes your registration in the current server; it is not a complete account-deletion command. The shared Bungie link and historical ticket/event records remain unless separately removed.

For support, corrections or a data-deletion request, contact d2embot@outlook.com. We may ask for proportionate confirmation that you control the relevant Discord account. Never send a password, bot token or authentication code.
